10 Password Security Rules

  1. Use a unique password for every account.
  2. Prefer longer passwords or passphrases.
  3. Avoid predictable personal information (names, years).
  4. Avoid common passwords and top-1000 lists.
  5. Never reuse passwords across sites.
  6. Use a password manager.
  7. Enable MFA wherever possible.
  8. Never share your password — not even with "support".
  9. Be cautious of phishing pages and lookalike domains.
  10. Change passwords when compromise is suspected or confirmed.

Password strength is only one part of authentication security. Combine it with MFA, rate limiting, secure hashing, session security and monitoring.
